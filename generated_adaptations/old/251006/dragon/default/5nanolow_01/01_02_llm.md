Reasoning and adaptation strategy:
- Review of the rules: All Warriors should go to the Cave to attack the Dragon. Farmers stay in the Village to farm and to help spawn new villagers when possible. There are two spawn groups: "spawn farmer" and "spawn warrior". For every two villagers assigned to a spawn group, and given enough wheat (10 for farmers, 12 for warriors), a new villager of the appropriate type is spawned. Spawning consumes villagers in the spawn group for the purpose of triggering spawns and uses wheat from the Farm.
- Observations: The Dragon fights back unpredictably, so it’s best to keep at least some villagers in the Cave to withstand potential dragon hits, but the problem statement enforces that all Warriors should go to the Cave to attack. Farmers are the key to generating wheat to enable spawning. The strategy should maximize early Dragon damage while ensuring enough wheat remains to spawn new villagers to replace losses and sustain the attack.
- Strategy outline:
  1) In assign_in_village:
     - Put all Farmers in the "farm" group by default (they stay in the village).
     - Put all Warriors in the "cave" group (they go to attack the Dragon directly from the Village side by moving to Cave).
     - Create optional spawn groups only if wheat is sufficient and there are enough villagers to pair for spawning:
       - Determine how many pairs of Farmers can be assigned to "spawn farmer" given wheat in environment.farm.wheat (need 10 wheat per pair).
       - Determine how many pairs of Warriors can be assigned to "spawn warrior" given wheat (need 12 wheat per pair).
       - Move those 2*spawn_pairs_farmer farmers to "spawn farmer" and 2*spawn_pairs_warrior warriors to "spawn warrior" accordingly, prioritizing using surplus Farmers first for spawning.
       - This step preserves all remaining Farmers in "farm" and all Warriors (not in spawn groups) in "cave".
  2) In assign_in_cave:
     - All Warriors in the Cave move to the "attack" group to attack the Dragon.
     - All Farmers in the Cave (if any exist due to initial distribution or spawns) should stay in the Cave only if you want them to help sustain the group; but the problem states all Warriors go to the Cave and then attack; Farmers can stay or move to Cave depending on the spawn decisions. To align with the instruction to keep Warriors in the Cave, we’ll place any non-Warrior Cave villagers in the "cave" group as well if they are currently there; otherwise, we keep them in "village" (which won’t be used for farming in this phase). We also add a "village" group to allow any villagers to move back if needed (not strictly required but keeps clarity).
- The strategy aims to maximize immediate Dragon damage from Warriors while ensuring a path to replenishment via spawning using the Farm’s wheat. It respects the requirement that every component is assigned to exactly one group in each phase.
