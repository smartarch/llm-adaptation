The previous version could assign `eat` without enough wheat, leaving the villager unassigned, and its high wheat threshold made Farmer spawns unlikely. This version eats only when wheat is available, explicitly sends everyone else to a valid group, and schedules two Farmer spawn events alongside Warrior spawns. Cave Warriors begin rotating back at age 3 so they can reach the Village in time to eat; when wheat is short, a hungry villager farms rather than being assigned an invalid meal.
