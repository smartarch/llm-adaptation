Reasoning and updated adaptation strategy:
- The previous implementation had a syntax error and relied on uncertain mechanisms to track current groups. The updated strategy uses a deterministic, straightforward approach:
  - In the village step:
    - All Warriors stay in or move to the Cave (attack group will be assigned in the cave step; here we ensure Warriors are not left idle in the village).
    - All Farmers stay in the Village by default (farm group).
    - Use Farmers to spawn new villagers via two spawn groups:
      - spawn farmer requires 2 farmers in that group and 10 wheat.
      - spawn warrior requires 2 farmers in that group and 12 wheat.
    - Allocation order:
      - First allocate as many farming Farmers to "spawn farmer" as possible given the wheat.
      - With remaining Farmers and remaining wheat, allocate to "spawn warrior" as many as possible.
  - In the cave step:
    - All Warriors attack the Dragon (group "attack").
    - Farmers go to the Village (group "village").

This approach is deterministic, avoids querying dynamic state, and respects the rules for spawning and group assignments. It should reduce risk of syntax errors and improve robustness.
