Reasoning and adaptation strategy:
- Objective: Kill the Dragon as fast as possible. All Warriors should go to the Cave to attack the Dragon. Farmers should stay in the Village to farm and to create additional villagers (spawn) when possible.
- Village strategy:
  - Default policy: put all Farmers in the "farm" group (they stay in the Village and farm).
  - Warriors: move them to the Cave by assigning them to the "cave" group.
  - Spawn logic: provide an opportunistic spawn mechanism to increase population when wheat reserves allow. If there are at least 4 Farmers in the Village and the Farm has enough wheat (for example wheat >= 20), reallocate two of the Farmers to a "spawn farmer" group while keeping the rest farming. This creates the condition "for every two villagers in spawn farmer and 10 wheat, a new Farmer is spawned" observed by the game, potentially increasing population over time. The exact numbers can be tuned; the baseline keeps farming as the primary task and uses spawning as a secondary growth mechanic when resources permit.
- Cave strategy:
  - Warriors attacked the Dragon: assign all Warriors present in the Cave to the "attack" group (they will attack the Dragon).
  - Farmers in the Cave are moved back to the Village by assigning them to the "village" group, since Farmers should stay in the Village.
  - The "cave" group in the cave holds any Farmers who are assigned to stay in the Cave if needed by the system, but the default policy prioritizes moving Farmers back to Village.
- Group validity: Use environment.assign_group(component, group_id) for each component, ensuring every component is assigned to exactly one of the allowed groups per the task.
