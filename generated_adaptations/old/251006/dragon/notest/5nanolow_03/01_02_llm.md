Reasoning and adaptation strategy:
- The goal is to quickly kill the Dragon while managing wheat and spawning new villagers to sustain combat.
- Rules extraction:
  - All Warriors should go to the Cave and attack the Dragon.
  - All Farmers stay in the Village and either farm or spawn new villagers (both Farmers and Warriors are necessary).
  - Spawns occur via two special groups in the Village: "spawn farmer" (needs 2 villagers in the group and 10 wheat to spawn a new Farmer) and "spawn warrior" (needs 2 villagers in the group and 12 wheat to spawn a new Warrior).
- Observations:
  - Spawns depend on the number of villagers assigned to the spawn groups and the available wheat in the Farm.
  - To keep things simple and consistent across steps, we should aggressively use Farmers to spawn new villagers as long as there is enough wheat, while keeping all existing Warriors in the Cave to attack.
  - In the village step:
    - Move all Warriors to the "cave" group (Go to the Cave).
    - Keep Farmers in the Village by default in the "farm" group.
    - Use some Farmers to populate "spawn farmer" and "spawn warrior" groups based on available wheat, thereby creating new Farmers/Warriors over time.
  - In the cave step:
    - Move all present Warriors to the "attack" group to attack the Dragon.
    - Move any Farmers (should be none in practice if we follow the strategy) to the "village" group to return to the Village.

  - Assign all Warriors to the "attack" group.
  - Assign any Farmers to the "village" group (they should return to the Village, though in practice there should be few if any).
