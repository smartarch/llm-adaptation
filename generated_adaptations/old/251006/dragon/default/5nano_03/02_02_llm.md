Reasoning and updated adaptation strategy:
- Observations from the failed run show that large numbers of villagers ended up in the village with minimal attacking pressure, and the dragon managed to eliminate many due to its AoE in the cave. A strong, robust strategy should reduce the number of villagers exposed to the dragon’s AoE while still delivering sufficient DPS to kill the dragon before turn 30.
- Core ideas:
  - Keep the cave population small and controlled (a small, fixed number of Warriors in cave at any time) to minimize the dragon’s AoE casualties.
  - Attack in waves: send a small number of Warriors to cave to attack, then rotate in new Warriors spawned over time so the total DPS increases gradually without risking mass casualties in one wave.
  - Use wheat aggressively to spawn new villagers in a managed way: spawn one Warrior when enough wheat and at least two Farmers exist; otherwise, spawn a Farmer to boost wheat production for future waves.
  - Farmers stay in the village to farm and maintain wheat production; Warriors are the ones that travel to the cave to attack.
  - In the cave, only Warriors should attack; Farmers in cave should move back to the village.

Adaptation strategy details:
- In assign_in_village:
  - Always keep up to two Warriors assigned to the cave group (these will be the current wave of attackers).
  - If there are at least two Farmers and at least 12 wheat, spawn a Warrior by assigning two Farmers to the "spawn warrior" group (one Warrior spawned this turn). If not possible but at least two Farmers and 10 wheat exist, spawn a Farmer via "spawn farmer" (to boost wheat production).
  - Any remaining Farmers are assigned to the "farm" group to continue producing wheat.
  - All other Warriors beyond the two-wielder cave wave are kept farming or prepared for future waves (assigned to "farm" unless we have enough wheat to spawn more Warriors).
- In assign_in_cave:
  - Warriors currently in the cave go to the "attack" group to strike the Dragon.
  - Farmers in the cave go to the "village" group to return to farming.

This approach reduces per-turn risk to the field by limiting cave casualties to a small, rotating group of attackers, while ramping up DPS over time through spawned Warriors. It also keeps the wheat production engine intact by ensuring farmers stay in the village and spawn decisions are only taken when resources allow.

from generated_adaptations.base_classes.dragon import DragonHuntAdaptation

class SmartAdaptation(DragonHuntAdaptation):
    def __init__(self, **kwargs):
        super().__init__(**kwargs)

    def assign_in_village(self, components, environment, group_ids, step: int):
        # Split villagers by role
        farmers = [c for c in components if c.role == "Farmer"]
        warriors = [c for c in components if c.role == "Warrior"]

        assigned = set()

        #  spawn decisions based on available wheat and villagers
        wheat = getattr(environment.farm, "wheat", 0)

        # Try to spawn a Warrior if possible (needs two villagers and 12 wheat)
        if len(farmers) >= 2 and wheat >= 12:
            spawn_targets = farmers[:2]
            for f in spawn_targets:
                environment.assign_group(f, "spawn warrior")
                assigned.add(f)
        # If cannot spawn Warrior, try to spawn a Farmer (needs two villagers and 10 wheat)
        elif len(farmers) >= 2 and wheat >= 10:
            spawn_targets = farmers[:2]
            for f in spawn_targets:
                environment.assign_group(f, "spawn farmer")
                assigned.add(f)

        # After potential spawns, assign current cave wave (up to 2 Warriors) and rest to farm
        cave_slots = min(2, len(warriors))
        cave_assigned = 0

        # Assign Warriors to cave (current wave)
        for w in warriors:
            if w in assigned:
                continue
            if cave_assigned < cave_slots:
                environment.assign_group(w, "cave")
                cave_assigned += 1
                assigned.add(w)
            else:
                environment.assign_group(w, "farm")
                assigned.add(w)

        # Remaining Farmers go to farming unless they were assigned to spawn already
        for f in farmers:
            if f in assigned:
                continue
            # If we didn't spawn Warrior or Farmer this turn, stay farming
            environment.assign_group(f, "farm")
            assigned.add(f)

    def assign_in_cave(self, components, environment, group_ids, step: int):
        # In the cave, Warriors should attack; Farmers should go back to the village
        for c in components:
            if c.role == "Warrior":
                environment.assign_group(c, "attack")
            else:
                environment.assign_group(c, "village")
